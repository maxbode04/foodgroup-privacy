Food Group — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-26

Food Group is built and operated by Maximilien Bode ("we", "us") in Sydney, Australia. This policy explains what data the app handles and your rights over it.

TL;DR. Food Group runs entirely on your iPhone. We do not collect, send, or store your personal data anywhere outside of your own device. What you enter — what you eat, your weight, your goals — never leaves your iPhone unless you explicitly export or share it.

What data the app processes

When you use Food Group, the app processes:

  • Your body dimensions: height, sex, birth date, activity level
  • Your goals: target weight, weekly pace, daily macro and micronutrient targets
  • Your food logs: which foods you ate, when, and how much
  • Your custom foods, scanned barcodes, and recipes
  • Your weight entries over time
  • Your app preferences: theme, reminder times

All of this is stored locally in the app's private sandbox on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework. No third party (including us) can access it.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect or transmit any usage analytics
  • We do not use third-party tracking, advertising, or marketing tools
  • We do not share your data with any external service
  • We do not require you to create an account or log in
  • We do not transmit your scanned barcodes to a community database

Network use

Food Group works offline. The only data that leaves your device is anonymous and contains nothing personal:

  • Open Food Facts lookups: when you scan a barcode or search packaged products, the app sends the barcode number or search text to the Open Food Facts database to fetch nutrition info. No personal data is sent.
  • App announcements: on launch the app fetches a small public file of announcements (such as beta or release notices). This is an anonymous request with no identifiers attached.

The app can read energy data (active and resting) from Apple's Health app to estimate your daily energy expenditure. This is read-only — the app writes nothing back to Health — and the data stays on your device. Apple controls this access and you can revoke it at any time.

Planned for a future version:

  • Cloud sync via Apple iCloud / CloudKit — encrypted and kept within your own Apple ID. Apple controls it and you can disable it.

In-app purchases

Food Group offers optional paid features via Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple handles payment, billing, and any associated personal data per Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your payment details.

Your rights

You can:

  • Export your data: Settings → Backup & data → "Export backup" produces a JSON file you can save or share anywhere.
  • Restore your data: Settings → Backup & data → "Restore from backup".
  • Delete your data: Settings → Backup & data → "Delete my data + account" removes every record from your device.

Uninstalling the app also removes all data.

Children

Food Group is rated 4+ but is designed for adults tracking their own nutrition. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

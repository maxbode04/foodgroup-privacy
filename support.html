Common questions

  • Where is my data stored? Entirely on your iPhone — nothing is sent to a server. See the Privacy Policy for details.
  • How do I back up or move my data? Settings → Backup & data → "Export backup" saves a portable file. "Restore from backup" brings it back after a reinstall.
  • Is it free? Food Group is free during the beta. A subscription will arrive at the full launch; anything you log now carries over.
  • Where does the nutrition data come from? The Australian Food Composition Database (AFCD), USDA, NHMRC reference values, and Open Food Facts — all credited in Settings → About → Credits.

A note on health

Food Group is a tracking tool, not medical advice. For dietary or medical decisions, please see your GP or an Accredited Practising Dietitian.
